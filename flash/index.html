Quick Troubleshooting:
• Make sure your USB cable is a Data cable (some thin charging cables cannot transfer data).
• Check that the LED display green Data wire is connected to pin D4.
• Check that 5V (Red) and GND (White/Black) wires are securely connected.

Frequently Asked Questions

❓ What kind of USB cable do I need?
Use a standard micro-USB or USB-C data cable. Some thin cables from charging stations are power-only and cannot transfer data.
❓ Do I need to install any software or drivers?
No! Everything runs 100% inside your browser using Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, or Opera on Windows, Mac, Linux, or Chromebooks.
❓ How do future updates work?
Once your FerryLight is connected to your home Wi-Fi, it will automatically check for and install updates over the air without needing USB again.
