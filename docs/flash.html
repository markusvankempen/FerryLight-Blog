❓ Quick Help & Tips

What kind of USB cable do I need?
Use a micro-USB or USB-C data cable. Thin charging-only cables cannot transfer firmware or settings.
Do I need to install any drivers?
No! Works 100% inside Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, or Opera on Windows, Mac, Linux, or Chromebooks.
Which pin does the LED display wire connect to?
Green (Data) connects to D4, Red connects to 5V, and Black/White connects to GND.
