FerryLight Web Flasher

Install or update FerryLight firmware on your ESP8266 / Wemos D1 Mini directly from your web browser over USB.

Your browser does not support Web Serial. Please use Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Opera, or Brave on desktop. Web Serial requires HTTPS or localhost. Ensure you are visiting this page via HTTPS.

No drivers, Python, or command-line tools needed.

How to Flash Your Display

  1. Connect ESP8266 via USB: Plug your Wemos D1 Mini or NodeMCU board into your computer with a USB data cable.
  2. Click "Connect & Install FerryLight": A browser dialog will appear listing available USB serial devices.
  3. Select Your Serial Port: Choose your board's COM / USB serial port (e.g. USB Serial, ch341-uart, or CP2102) and click Connect.
  4. Wait for Flashing to Complete: The installer will erase and write the firmware binary, then reset the board.
  5. Wi-Fi Setup: Once flashed, join the open Wi-Fi network FerryLight-... on your phone/computer and open http://192.168.4.1 to configure your home Wi-Fi and MQTT.
